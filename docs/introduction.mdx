<Info>

</Info>

## A day in the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Morning huddle">
    Open **[Home](/features/home)**: the personalized dashboard shows today's alerts, what's assigned to you, the day's calendar, and the members who need attention right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear today's urgent work">
    Jump into the worklist that names your job, **[TCM](/features/tcm-worklist)** for post-discharge follow-ups, **[TOC](/features/toc-worklist)** for the agent-led transition-of-care queue, **[HEDIS](/features/hedis-worklist)** for care gaps, **[HCC](/features/hcc-worklist)** for coding, **[CCM](/features/ccm-worklist)** for chronic care, **[AWV](/features/awv-worklist)** or **[JSA](/features/jsa-worklist)** for annual and on-site assessments. Bulk-assign the routine work to an AI agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor agents live">
    In **[Calls → Calling Agents](/features/calls)** watch live transcripts, transfer when it's the right thing to do, and step in when a member needs a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deep-dive a member">
    Open the **[Patient Profile](/features/patient-profile)**: clinical history and outreach on the left, Care Programs and Care Management on the right, with the P360 banner (acuity, consent, next appointment, care team) across the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the month">
    Run **[CCM](/features/ccm-worklist)** or **[APCM Billing](/features/apcm-billing)** month-close: attest, verify eligibility, export the claim batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report and steer">
    Open **[Analytics](/features/analytics)**, filter to a practice and period, drill into anomalies, and export a slide for leadership. Send a cohort back into a worklist to act on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes Fold Health different

- **One workflow language across every worklist.** Learn one; you know them all. Filters, bulk actions, member cell, Quick View, identical from TOC to HCC to HEDIS.
- **AI agents are part of the platform, not a bolt-on.** The same table that shows your queue shows the queue an agent is working. Live transcripts. Human hand-off. One audit log for the whole conversation.
- **The member is the record.** Every worklist entry, every gap, every call, every task rolls up to one patient profile. No orphan tickets, no separate CRM.
- **Compliance is a workflow, not a spreadsheet.** MEAT evidence in HCC, consent in CCM, attestation in APCM, HRA in SNP, enforced by the UI at the moment the work happens.
- **Every decision is auditable.** Every worklist state change, agent flow update, and setting change is logged with the who and the when.
