The **Calendar** is a full-page scheduling surface. It's the same appointment data the Home page's Today's Calendar card renders, but here you have the full toolbar, view switcher, and multi-day layouts.

Route: `#/calendar`.

## Views

- **Day**: 24-hour column with appointment blocks and a live "Now" line.
- **Week**: 7-day grid, useful for provider schedules and finding open slots.
- **Month**: high-level grid; click any day to jump to Day view for that date.

Switch views with the **Day / Week / Month** toggle in the toolbar.

## Walkthrough: schedule an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a date">
    Use the toolbar's date picker or click any day-cell in Month view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click an empty time slot">
    In Day/Week view, click on an unfilled row. The **Schedule Drawer** opens pre-filled with the selected date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the appointment">
    Pick patient (searchable), provider, location, and appointment type. Notes are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The appointment writes to Supabase and appears on the calendar, on the patient's profile, and on the Home card immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Walkthrough: batch-schedule from a worklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="From AWV / JSA / any worklist">
    Select rows and use BulkBar → **Schedule Batch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the wizard">
    The wizard fits the selected patients into available slots for the target provider and location, honoring their preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit">
    Approving commits every appointment to the calendar in one write; changes surface immediately on Day view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Toolbar

- **Date picker**: jump to any date.
- **View switcher**: Day / Week / Month.
- **Provider filter**: scope to one provider's slots.
- **Location filter**: scope to one clinic/site.
- **+ New Appointment**: opens the Schedule Drawer without a pre-filled slot.

## Tips

- **The "Now" line** updates every minute in Day view, useful during handoff calls.
- **Drag to move**: grab an appointment block and drop it on a new slot; a confirm dialog protects against accidental moves.
- **Deep links**: `#/calendar?date=2026-08-14&view=week` opens the calendar on a specific date and view.
