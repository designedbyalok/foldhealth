The **JSA Worklist** manages **Joint Site Assessments**, the on-site and in-home visit queue for field teams. It shares the AWV worklist's shape: scheduling-first, provider/location aware, and outreach-batch friendly.

Route: `#/population/jsa`.

## Columns

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Name, gender, age, member ID, language |
| **Assessment Status** | Not scheduled / Scheduled / Completed |
| **Due** | JSA deadline; overdue rows chip red |
| **Assessor** | The field clinician or team assigned |
| **Location** | Home address or site |
| **Insurance** | Plan (drives billing rules) |
| **Assignee** | Scheduler |
| **Actions** | Schedule, call, open profile |

## Walkthrough: plan a field day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open JSA">
    Click **JSA** in the SubNav.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by assessor and radius">
    Pick your **Assessor** and use the **Location** filter to scope to a ZIP or region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the day's visits">
    Check the members you want to see in one route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Batch-schedule">
    BulkBar → **Schedule Batch**: the wizard fits selected members into available slots on the Calendar with drive-time estimates between locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and hand off">
    Confirm the plan; the schedule commits to the Calendar and the field clinician sees it on their day view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **JSA rows carry the home address**, not just clinic, useful for door-to-door routing.
- **Completed assessments unlock billing**: mark complete from the Quick View or from the Calendar block after the visit.
