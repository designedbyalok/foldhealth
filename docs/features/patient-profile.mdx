The **Patient Profile** is the deep view of a single patient. It's split into two synchronized panes so a clinician can review clinical context on the left while managing care programs on the right, no tab-switching cost.

Route pattern: `#/population/{worklist}/patient/{memberId}` (or `#/hedis/patient/{memberId}`).

## Opening a profile

- **From any worklist row**: click the patient name; the Quick View drawer opens. Click **Open Profile** in the drawer header to expand into the full two-pane view.
- **From Home**: Alerts & Monitoring card → click a patient.
- **From Calls**: click a patient in the call history left panel.
- **From a deep link**: every profile URL is shareable.

The **P360 banner** spans both panes at the top: acuity/RAF chip, consent status, next appointment, chronic conditions row, vitals summary, family, and care team. Expand it to see per-appointment detail and a full care-team card.

## Left-pane tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Snapshot: demographics, insurance, PCP, chronic conditions, recent activity |
| **PAMI/Hx** | Problem list, Allergies, Medications, Immunizations, and clinical History |
| **Vitals & Labs** | Time-series vitals with sparklines, and a labs table with trend arrows |
| **Care Gaps** | Every open HEDIS/HCC gap; click a row to open the closure workflow |
| **Outreach** | Full outreach timeline, calls, messages, letters, filtered by direction and status |
| **Comms** | Chat threads with the patient (SMS, secure messaging, email replies) |
| **Tasks** | Every task linked to this patient (create, edit, complete) |

## Right-pane tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Program-level summary: which programs, current status, next steps |
| **Care Programs** | The editable **Care Programs table**, Status dropdown (Engaged / Declined / Unable to Reach / Enrolled / Attempted), Assignee picker, three-dot row menu (Assign / Print Summary / Close Program), and a pie-fill completion ring per program |
| **Care Management** | Care plan authoring, goals, and interdisciplinary team notes |

## Walkthrough: patient prep before a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    From the worklist row, open Quick View → **Open Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the banner">
    RAF, consent, chronic conditions, next appointment, all at the top. Expand for full detail (appointments column, care team card).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read PAMI/Hx and Vitals">
    Left pane → **PAMI/Hx** for the problem list and med list. Switch to **Vitals & Labs** for recent BP, HbA1c, weight sparklines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check open gaps and outreach">
    Left pane → **Care Gaps** to see what's due. Switch to **Outreach** for the last three call attempts and dispositions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Care Programs on the right">
    Right pane → **Care Programs**. Which programs is the patient on? Any past enrollments closed? What's next in the current program's step list?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the call">
    From the banner header, click **Call**. The call opens with everything you just read still on screen, no context loss.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Walkthrough: enroll a patient in a Care Program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Right pane → Care Programs">
    The table shows every program available for this patient's plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Status">
    In the Status column, pick **Enrolled** from the dropdown. Enrolling stamps the **Start Date** with today automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a care manager">
    Click the **Assignee** cell: a searchable picker (`RoleAssigneePicker`) opens. Pick a user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    The **Completion ring** (pie fill) shows how many program steps have been completed. Click into the row → **Steps** to check off individual milestones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close when done">
    Row ⋯ menu → **Close Program** flips the status to Enrolled (Closed) with a close reason and locks the step list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deep-linking

Every tab, subtab, and drawer serializes into the URL:

- `#/population/toc/patient/M12345`, TOC context, member M12345, default tab
- `#/population/tcm/patient/M12345`, TCM context, member M12345, default tab
- `#/population/toc/patient/M12345/care-gaps`, jump straight to the Care Gaps tab
- `#/hedis/patient/M12345`, same member from the HEDIS context (preserves back-nav to HEDIS)

## Tips

- **The banner adapts.** P360 data is seeded for every member; hand-crafted rows survive the re-seed script (see `scripts/seed_p360_banner.js`).
- **Tabs remember scroll.** Switching tabs preserves scroll positions per-tab so long tables don't reset.
- **Care Programs row menu.** Hover a row to reveal the ⋯ menu, Assign / Print Summary / Close Program.
