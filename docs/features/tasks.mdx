The **Tasks** workspace is where every follow-up, call-back, chart request, or personal to-do lives. It has two synchronized views (List and Kanban) over the same underlying data.

## Views

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="List view" icon="list">
    Grouped by status (To Do, In Progress, Blocked, Done) with sortable columns for Title, Priority, Due Date, Assignee, and Labels. Best for triage and bulk edits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kanban view" icon="columns-3">
    Drag cards between status columns to update state. Best for standups and quick status changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Switch views with the **List / Kanban** toggle at the top-right.

## Walkthrough: create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Task drawer">
    Click **+ New Task** in the top-right of the Tasks page. A 700px right-side drawer slides in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the essentials">
    Type a **Title** (required). Add a **Description** in the rich-text field if the task needs context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set metadata">
    Pick a **Status** (defaults to To Do), **Priority** (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), **Due Date**, **Assignee**, and any **Labels**. Every field is a `FilterChip`, searchable, single-select unless it says otherwise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach to a patient (optional)">
    Link the task to a patient with the **Patient** field. The task then shows up on that patient's Tasks tab as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add subtasks">
    Break the work down in the **Subtasks** section. Each subtask has its own checkbox and can be checked off without opening the parent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Task**. The task lands in the current view and, if assigned to you, on the Home card **Assigned to Me**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Walkthrough: work through your queue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter to yours">
    Use the **Assignee** FilterChip to scope the view to your user, or use the **My Tasks** quick filter at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a task">
    Click a row (List) or a card (Kanban). The **Task Detail Drawer** opens with three sections: Details, Subtasks, and Activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update inline">
    Every field in the Details section is editable in place, no separate "edit" mode. Changes save on blur.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log progress">
    Comment in the **Activity** feed. Every state change (status, assignee, due date) is auto-logged alongside your comments, so the whole trail is in one place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark done">
    Set status to **Done** (or drag the card to the Done column in Kanban). The task collapses out of active views but stays searchable and appears on the patient's Tasks tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **Bulk edit**: select multiple rows in List view to change status, assignee, or priority for the whole selection.
- **Keyboard**: `Cmd+K` opens task search; `N` opens the Add Task drawer from anywhere in Tasks.
- **Patient tasks**: a task created from a patient profile's Tasks tab is pre-linked to that patient and inherits their assigned care manager as the default assignee.
