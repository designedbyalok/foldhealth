The **Home** page is a customizable dashboard powered by `react-grid-layout`. It gives every user a personal "morning huddle" view, weather, patient alerts, today's calendar, and the items assigned to them, without touching a worklist.

## The default layout

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome** | Greeting with your name, your location (geolocation + reverse geocoding), and current weather from Open-Meteo |
| **Alerts & Monitoring** | Patient rows pulled from Supabase with badges for Missed calls, unread Messages, active Alerts, and open Tasks |
| **Assigned to Me** | Recent interactions and items assigned to your user account |
| **Today's Calendar** | 24-hour day view with appointment blocks, a live "Now" line, and click-to-schedule |
| **Quick Notes** | Sticky-note style cards with free text and timestamps |

## Walkthrough: customize your dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Click **Home** in the sidebar (top item). The default layout loads on first visit and is cached in `localStorage` after that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Click **Edit Dashboard** in the top-right. Drag handles appear on each card header, and a resize grip appears in the bottom-right corner of each card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rearrange">
    Drag any card by its header to a new position on the 12-column grid. Cards snap and reflow as you drop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resize">
    Drag the bottom-right corner of any card to change width and height. The grid honors minimum sizes so cards never collapse below usable dimensions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or reset">
    Click **Done** to commit the layout, it's written to `localStorage` and restored on your next visit. Or click **Reset** to restore the default layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Walkthrough: schedule from the calendar card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an open slot">
    The calendar card shows today's appointments as positioned blocks. The red "Now" line updates every minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click an empty time slot">
    Clicking an unfilled row on the day-view opens the **Schedule Drawer** pre-populated with the selected date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the appointment">
    Pick a patient, provider, location, and appointment type. Click **Save**, the appointment writes to Supabase and appears immediately on both the Home card and the full [Calendar](/features/calendar).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **Layout is per-user, per-browser.** Layouts are stored in `localStorage`; if a user clears storage or opens the app on a new machine, they see the default until they customize again.
- **Weather uses your OS location.** The Welcome card requests browser geolocation once, deny it and you'll see a generic greeting with no weather.
- **Cards use live data.** Alerts, Assigned to Me, and Today's Calendar all subscribe to Supabase, no refresh needed after a task is completed or an appointment is added.
