## Walkthrough: investigate a dip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start at the Executive view">
    Open **Analytics** in the sidebar; the URL is `#/analytics/executive`. Scan the KPI row for anything unexpected, e.g. "TCM completion dropped from 74% to 61% this month."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the period">
    Use the **Period selector** in the toolbar to switch from "Last 90 days" to just the month in question. Every chart re-queries and re-renders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope to a practice">
    Add a **Practice** filter to see whether one clinic drove the change. If yes, keep that filter as you dig deeper.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drill down">
    Click the dropping bar directly. If the chart supports drill-down (most Executive and Financial charts do), it opens a detail popover with the underlying cohort. Click **View patients** to send that cohort to the Population worklist as a filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cross-reference">
    Switch to a specialized view: **Utilization** if the dip is about follow-ups, **Care Coordination** if it's about hand-offs, **AI Analytics** if it's about agent outcomes. All filters carry over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Use the toolbar **Export** button to download the current chart (PNG) or its underlying data (CSV) for a slide or a follow-up email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common controls

Every view has the same top toolbar:

- **Period selector**: presets (Today, 7d, 30d, 90d, YTD, All) + custom range picker.
- **Practice filter**: single-select or multi-select depending on the view; scoped to the practices your account can see.
- **Drill-down**: click on chart marks (bars, segments, points) to open a cohort popover.
- **Export**: download the current chart as PNG or its data as CSV.

## Tips

- **URL is the state.** Every filter, period, and drill-down writes to the hash, share a link and the recipient lands on exactly what you're looking at.
- **Charts are built with Recharts.** All colors come from `src/tokens/tokens.css`, the dashboards adapt automatically when tokens change.
- **Simulator caveats.** The **ROI Simulator** is a "what if" tool: inputs never persist and don't affect real cohorts. It's a modeling scratchpad.
