The **Annual Visit (AWV) Worklist** is the scheduling queue for Medicare Annual Wellness Visits. AWVs generate risk assessments and preventive plans (and are a Medicare-covered visit): but only if scheduled. The worklist surfaces every member due, colored by how overdue they are.

Route: `#/population/awv`.

## Columns

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Name, gender, age, member ID, language |
| **Last AWV** | Date of last completed AWV, or "Never" |
| **Due** | Due date; overdue rows show a red chip |
| **Provider** | Preferred/assigned provider for the visit |
| **Location** | Preferred/assigned clinic |
| **Chronic Conditions** | ICD chips, informs the visit prep |
| **Insurance** | Plan (for eligibility confirmation) |
| **Assignee** | Scheduler / care manager |
| **Actions** | Schedule, place a call, open profile |

## Walkthrough: book overdue AWVs for a provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Annual Visit">
    Click **Annual Visit** in the Population SubNav (URL: `#/population/awv`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by provider and status">
    Open the **Provider** filter chip and pick your provider. Open **Status** and pick **Overdue** (or **Due within 30d** for gentler outreach).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by location if needed">
    If the provider works at multiple sites, add the **Location** chip too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bulk-select and reach out">
    Check the header checkbox; the BulkBar surfaces **Add to Campaign**, **Assign**, and **Schedule Batch**.
    - **Add to Campaign**: send an AWV outreach email/SMS to everyone selected.
    - **Schedule Batch**: opens a wizard that walks through slots and drops appointments straight into the [Calendar](/features/calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Per-row scheduling">
    For high-touch members, open the row → **Schedule** from the Quick View header. The Schedule Drawer opens pre-filled with member, provider, and location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **The Due column drives urgency.** Rows sort by Due ascending by default, the most overdue members are at the top.
- **Provider and Location filters are searchable.** Type-ahead works on both, useful when a practice has 40+ providers.
- **Not all AWVs are Medicare.** Some payers reimburse similar visits; the worklist includes those and shows the plan-specific requirements in Quick View.
